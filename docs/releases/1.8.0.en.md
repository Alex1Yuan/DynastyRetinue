# Dynasty Retinue & Refit 1.8.0

- Adds an AI-controlled escort fleet with Sword frigates, prow-lance frigates, cruisers and grand cruisers. Manage each ship's name, loadout and upgrades individually.
- Fleet rosters, names, refits, upgrades and acquired equipment records are stored with each game save. Loading an earlier save restores the fleet at that point, including within the same campaign.
- Retinue Management has separate Ground Retinue and Escort Fleet pages, with Roster and Recruit tabs for the fleet. The Shipyard handles flagship refits. Each ship's equipment section starts collapsed and can be expanded independently. Select equipment by name from expandable lists, and view PF as used/limit.
- Fleet costs reserve Profit Factor without spending it. Fleet capacity is 6; frigates, cruisers and grand cruisers use 1, 2 and 3 capacity respectively.
- Configure extra shots and range individually for cruisers and grand cruisers, with affected weapon counts and cost formulas shown in the UI. Existing fleets remain available when over budget; dismissals and changes that do not increase costs remain allowed.
- Refit with compatible ship equipment recorded as acquired in the current campaign, without moving or consuming inventory items. Original loadouts remain available. Weapon AI uses validated slot and role mappings while the selected weapon retains its own damage, range, ammunition and effects.
- Refit plasma drives, void shield generators, auger arrays and armour plating. Restore original components and retain installed equipment when loading a combat save.
- Improves repeated fire, retargeting after a kill and opportunities to use remaining shots farther along the AI's existing route.
- Fixes several attack and combat-log errors after a ship target is destroyed, and handles duplicate Rapid Reload registration caused by extra shots.
- Improves retinue and shipyard cost explanations, button labels, title visibility and English UI text.
- Extends the melee-elite low-health self-damage guard to Tactician Fervour alongside Blood Oath and Blade Shroud. The default threshold blocks these abilities below 50% HP and remains configurable.

Default costs: 10/20/30 PF per frigate/cruiser/grand cruiser hull; 2 PF per modified open slot; 2 PF per additional shot per affected weapon; 1 PF per additional range cell per affected weapon. Ground retinue PF limits remain separate.

The prow-lance frigate uses its original dorsal macro battery and prow lance. The Sword frigate retains port and starboard batteries. Torpedoes and attack craft are outside this release's selectable refit catalogue. Automatic flagship turn ending is a single-player developer test option, disabled by default.
