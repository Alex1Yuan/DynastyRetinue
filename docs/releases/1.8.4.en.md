# Dynasty Retinue & Refit 1.8.4

- Fixes regular Suppress guards inheriting an NPC “On Position” effect that prevented movement and made them display 0 MP at the start of their turns.
- New recruits are fixed immediately. Existing guards are repaired when loading a ground-area save, entering a ground area or deploying from reserve. No dismissal or recruitment reset is needed; the same guard, name, level and owned items are retained.
- Removes only this NPC template's stationary effect. Other immobilization effects remain effective, and vanilla enemies and story NPCs are unchanged.
- Preserves native movement points without refilling spent resources. Uses existing recruitment and restoration events, with no new per-frame scanning.
