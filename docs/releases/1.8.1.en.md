# Dynasty Retinue & Refit 1.8.1

- **Hull selection:** Choose a ship class, then a hull variant before recruiting. Frigates, cruisers and grand cruisers each offer two variants with an original-equipment preview.
- **Two new hulls:** Adds the pirate Dictator-class cruiser and Chaos battlecruiser with their original weapons, AI and strike craft. The pirate Dictator also retains its low-health retreat behaviour.
- **Full ship previews:** View the complete textured hull alongside weapons by slot, four main components and original special abilities.
- **UI fixes:** Fixes missing large-ship previews and blank or clipped equipment text. Adds a visible scrollbar.
- **Save compatibility:** Existing fleets remain compatible. PF costs and fleet capacity are unchanged, and the update archive does not overwrite personal settings.

Both frigate variants use the original Sword model with different weapon layouts. Torpedoes and strike craft remain outside the refit catalogue.

Verified in this update: previews, recruitment, carrier weapon fire, strike-craft launches and rewards for the tested encounter. Strike-craft attacks, return, combat-save restoration, final cleanup and muzzle alignment on some hulls remain under testing. Two-client co-op has not yet been tested for this update. Logged area-damage and post-battle voice exceptions also remain under investigation; please attach an exported diagnostic report when reporting problems.
