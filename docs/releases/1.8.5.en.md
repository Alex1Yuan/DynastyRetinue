# Dynasty Retinue & Refit 1.8.5

- **Optional guard friendly-fire protection:** A single switch under Combat & Behaviour is disabled by default. When enabled, it blocks health damage directly caused by allied guards to your party, other guards and allies, including burst and area damage through the same damage pipeline. Ricochets exclude non-enemy targets. Enemy attacks, player attacks and guards' own ability health costs retain their normal rules.
- **Guard psykers:** When enabled, the same switch also prevents guard casts from triggering automatic psychic phenomena or Perils of the Warp, including effects that would redirect onto someone else. Turning it off restores these native rules. The existing option preventing guard casts from increasing veil degradation remains independent. Player and enemy psykers retain their normal risks.
- **Simpler progression:** Adds **Attributes only** under Progression. Full builds remain the default; disabling Auto growth stops further career advancement. Attributes-only mode grants characteristic advances and equipment proficiencies without adding career abilities or other talents.
- **Existing guards:** Learned abilities are retained. Deliberately skipped ranks are not backfilled when full growth resumes. Choose the mode before recruiting for a fully simplified build; innate abilities and equipment effects remain.
- **Consistent stats after loading:** Fixes some NPC-based guards changing attributes and health after a cold load. Their native stat modifiers now refresh when the area is ready and when returning from reserve, in every growth mode.
- **Sound Constitution:** Its existing level-based health bonus updates immediately during attributes-only progression.
- **Performance and co-op:** New work runs on damage, casting, recruitment, growth and restoration events, with no new per-frame scene scan. The progression option is included in host-controlled guard transactions.

Friendly-fire protection requires the guard to remain the damage source. Environmental chain reactions with a different source and non-damage effects are outside that guarantee. Tested in the real single-player engine, including cold restart; this update has not been tested with two co-op clients.

The update archive does not include personal Settings.xml. Full progression remains the default.
