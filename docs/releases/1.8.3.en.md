# Dynasty Retinue & Refit 1.8.3

- Fixes low-level guards potentially receiving talents from career ranks they had not reached.
- Preserves normal progression and delayed talent grants once the relevant career rank and prerequisites are satisfied.
- Verified in-game recruitment at level 1, recruitment with player XP alignment, and level-1 recruitment after dismissal. The two level-1 recruits had identical talent, progression and equipment data, with no extra abilities carried over from the higher-level guard.

Existing saved features are not automatically removed. To rebuild an affected regular guard, dismiss and recruit that guard again after updating; its old name, XP and equipment instances will not carry over. Innate unit abilities and equipment-tier rules are unchanged.
