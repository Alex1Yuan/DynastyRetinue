# Dynasty Retinue & Refit 1.8.2

- **Reserve ground guards:** New individual and whole-retinue Reserve / Deploy controls let you leave guards behind before dangerous encounters without dismissing them.
- **Keep your existing guards:** Names, levels, equipment and elite identities are retained. Reserved guards still count towards recruitment limits. Switching does not charge a fee, heal guards or reissue gear.
- **Saved deployment choices:** Each save retains its own deployment state. Older saves default to all guards deployed. Automatic placement and stuck-unit rescue will not pull reserved guards back into the field.
- **Out-of-combat changes:** Switching is unavailable in combat, while downed or during an uninterruptible action. Co-op changes are initiated by the host through the existing synchronized command channel.
- **UI and logs:** Adds Chinese and English status labels, results and explanations, and simplifies the startup diagnostic message.

Single-player checks covered individual and group switching, a fresh game launch loading a reserve save, indoor/outdoor subarea travel, and real-save comparisons of guard identities, progression and equipment. Two-client co-op testing for this update remains pending.

The reported Linux/Proton thread-suspension crash still needs a failed-run log; this update does not claim to fix it. Strike-craft tests and muzzle alignment work remain separate follow-up tasks.
